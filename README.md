#ContactDex

### Basics
A responsive web application for managing business contacts. ContactDex helps professionals stay on top of their network by centralising contact details, interaction history, and follow-up tasks in one place.
Overview
Most professionals lose context on contacts over time - they forget the last conversation, miss follow-ups, and have no lightweight way to collaborate on shared contacts. ContactDex solves this with a clean, fast contact manager built for desktop and mobile.
Features
User Accounts
Email/password registration and login, Microsoft and Google SSO, password reset, and account deletion.
Contact Management
Add, view, edit, and delete contacts. Search across your contact list and track the last interaction date for each contact.
Notes
Record detailed interaction notes against individual contacts. View, edit, and delete notes over time to maintain a full interaction history.
Follow-Up and To-Do List
Create follow-up tasks linked to contacts, or standalone tasks. Auto-suggest follow-ups based on notes, mark contacts as no-follow-up-needed, and manage your full task backlog in one place.
Contact Sharing
Share contacts and follow-up tasks with other users in your organisation. Changes sync in real time so your team always has the latest information.
Sync
All contacts and tasks sync seamlessly across devices so your data is always current, whether you're at your desk or on the go.
Notifications
Get alerted when follow-up tasks are due, when a contact or task is shared with you, and when shared items are updated by a colleague.
